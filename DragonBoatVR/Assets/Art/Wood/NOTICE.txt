Diffuse and OpenGL normal images are the original 1K JPEG downloads.
The URP mask was derived from the original ARM image:
  R = 0 (nonmetal wood), G = source ambient occlusion, B = 0,
  A = 1 - source roughness. Scalar channels are imported as linear.
The base color uses sRGB. The normal uses Unity's normal-map importer.
No geometry, physics or sensor parameters are derived from this asset.
